# Kiddo.school art direction

Use the user's example prompt as a STYLE reference, never as a request to copy its subject, activity, brand or website.

- Preserve the established rainbow KIDDO.SCHOOL logo and Make · Learn · Discover branding.
- Pure white backgrounds, bright flat cartoon colours and recognizable useful illustrations.
- Small rounded child-friendly titles. The illustration is the main focus.
- No shadows, gradients, textures, decorative strokes, motion marks, splashes, sparkles, decorative borders, background scenery or filler objects in new artwork.
- Functional writing areas and educational content only when needed for that activity.
- Portrait assets use 2:3 dimensions. Display the full source image without cropping or stretching; preserve landscape assets at their own original ratio.
- Keep one worksheet preview per activity, and do not repeat the current activity among related cards.
- Do not regenerate existing artwork merely to implement website layout corrections.

- Functional cutting borders around printable cards and cutout shapes are requested.
