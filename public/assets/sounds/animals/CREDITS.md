# Animal sound recordings — sources and licenses

All recordings in this folder are real animal sounds, shipped with the
Kiddo.school static assets. Dog, cat, cow, sheep, bird, chicken, horse,
lion and pig recordings came with the original Animals & Their Sounds
class assets.

Duck and frog recordings were added from Wikimedia Commons under
CC BY-SA 4.0, trimmed and level-matched (loudnorm, mono 64 kbps MP3):

- `duck.mp3` — from "Pekin duck & mallard" by WaderClub,
  Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0,
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pekin_duck_%26_mallard.ogg
  (trimmed to 4.5 s, level-normalized)

- `frog.mp3` — from "Single Frog Croak" by MichaeltheFox8621,
  Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0,
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Single_Frog_Croak.oga
  (trimmed to 3.5 s, level-normalized)

Attribution for both is also shown to grown-ups on the
Magic Animal Playground page ("About the sounds" section).
