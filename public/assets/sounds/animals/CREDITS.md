# Animal sound recordings — sources and licenses

All recordings in this folder are real animal sounds, shipped with the
Kiddo.school static assets. Dog, cat, cow, sheep, bird, chicken, horse,
lion and pig recordings came with the original Animals & Their Sounds
class assets.

Duck and frog recordings were added from Wikimedia Commons,
trimmed and level-matched (loudnorm, mono 64 kbps MP3):

- `duck.mp3` — "Anas platyrhynchos - Mallard - XC62258" by Jonathon
  Jongsma, xeno-canto via Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0,
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Anas_platyrhynchos_-_Mallard_-_XC62258.ogg
  (a close-up recording of a female mallard giving the classic quack;
  trimmed to 2.6 s, level-normalized. Replaced the earlier far-field
  farm recording, which was too muddy to hear a clear quack.)

- `frog.mp3` — from "Single Frog Croak" by MichaeltheFox8621,
  Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0,
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Single_Frog_Croak.oga
  (trimmed to 3.5 s, level-normalized)

Attribution for both is also shown to grown-ups on the
Animal Sound Safari page ("About the sounds" section).
